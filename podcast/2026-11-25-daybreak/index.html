Lead Story

The Nasdaq resumed its selloff on Tuesday, with tech stocks stumbling again as fears around the AI trade crept back in.

It came one day after Jensen Huang's return at Nvidia sparked the best day for US stocks since April 2025.

Nvidia fell more than seven percent in the reversal. Anthropic dropped sharply and sank below its IPO price.

Those are the two poles of this year's AI trade — the incumbent hardware name, and the newest publicly traded frontier AI company.

One session apart, the tape gave the market its best day in more than a year, and then handed it back.

The reversal signals fragile sentiment toward the AI trade. It says nothing about orders, backlogs or capital spending — only about what investors are willing to pay for them.

That distinction matters for how you read the next few sessions. A demand problem shows up in guidance and cancellations. A sentiment problem shows up in the multiple.

The tape doesn't tell us why. It tells us what happened — a leadership group that rallied hard on Monday gave it back on Tuesday.

For a portfolio with heavy AI exposure, the concentration is the exposure. When the same few names carry the upside, a reversal in two of them is a portfolio event.

If the selling broadens beyond Nvidia and Anthropic, it reaches the semiconductor complex — memory, equipment, foundry — and from there the index itself.

The useful question now is whether this is a repricing of the story or the start of a broader de-rating. The tape hasn't answered it yet.

So the core fact, once more. The Nasdaq's selloff resumed Tuesday, led by Nvidia and Anthropic, one day after the best session for US stocks since April 2025.

Watch whether the selloff broadens into the broader semiconductor complex. That's the line between a two-name pullback and a sector repricing.
