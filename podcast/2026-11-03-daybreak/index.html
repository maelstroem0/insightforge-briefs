Lead Story

Brent remains far above roughly seventy dollars before the war began, so this is a repricing of the premium, not a return to normal. Lower crude, if sustained, eases inflation inputs and supports airlines and rate-sensitive assets while pressuring energy producers. The durability depends on a signed framework, not on signals alone. Washington and Tehran have both signaled room for diplomacy to end an eight-month war, but post-midterm escalation remains live.

For equities, the read-through runs through inflation and rates. A lower oil path gives cover to rate-sensitive sectors and consumers, while energy producers lose the scarcity bid that supported October earnings.
