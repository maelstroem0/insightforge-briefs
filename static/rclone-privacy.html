rclone-adham: Privacy Policy

"rclone-adham" is a private backup tool used only by its owner, Adham Azzam. It is not offered to anyone else.

It uses the open-source program rclone to copy the owner's own files from his own computers to his own Google Drive account.

Data

  • The tool accesses only the owner's Google Drive, with the owner's consent.
  • No data is collected, sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • Data obtained through Google APIs is stored only on the owner's own devices and in his own Google Drive.
  • The tool's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
