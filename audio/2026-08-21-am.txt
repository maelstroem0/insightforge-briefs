SECOND STORY

The strikes include the Ufa refining hub, about twelve hundred kilometers from the Ukrainian border. That hub contains three separate refineries. European diesel and gasoline cracks are bid, and energy supply risk has widened beyond the Middle East. The refinery loss is a separate shock from the Iran confrontation. It also puts a floor under refining margins. The Kremlin can still project a fictional battlefield performance to the public, but it cannot hide empty pumps. Watch for any additional Ukrainian long-range strikes on refining capacity and for Russian export restrictions. The core fact: Moscow is now living with the war's economic consequences.
