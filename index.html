AI's capex bill meets the credit market

The AI buildout stopped being an equity story this week and became a credit story. Alphabet and Amazon both missed and both burned cash; Intel's guidance blamed memory prices for a PC reset; the shares fell roughly in the order of their capex. The tell was the ex-AI index measurement: the S&P 500 without AI enablers was up 12.4% through Oct. 22 against 12% for the standard index. Meanwhile September core PCE at 3.1% year over year and a 10-2 Fed hold kept the December hike alive, so the financing that funds the buildout is getting dearer just as the returns slip. Moody's and Fitch warned on refinancing; OpenAI cut its raise; Anthropic arrived carrying an expected double-B rating. Brent settled at $117.64 and US debt reached 100% of GDP. Next week's AMD and Apple prints decide whether this is a de-rating or a repricing.
