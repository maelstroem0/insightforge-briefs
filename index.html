Long End and Oil Rule the Week

Audio edition ~12 min · the week, in one voice

The week belonged to the long end, with crude as its enforcer. Treasury tripled its buyback to $6 billion for 10-20 year maturities on Sept 10, and the 10-year Treasury yield still hit 4.841%, the highest since 2023. By Sept 12, August US CPI held at 3.4% year-over-year and Fed-hike odds were about 90% ahead of the Sept 15-16 FOMC. Oil made the inflation math worse: Brent November crude rose to $101.84 on Sept 11, and Houthis took Bab el-Mandeb by Sept 12. German 10-year yield topped 3.5% for the first time since April 2011. Equities could rally Friday—the Dow added 1.08%—but logged its worst week since March. AI's capex story survived via Oracle's backlog, but macro repriced everything. The market is no longer paying for duration or promises; it is demanding current yield and hard supply evidence.
