AI capex meets political and financing risk

The week's defining force was the collision of the AI capex trade with political and financing risk. Democrats' midterm sweep did not just change Washington; it repriced who permits, funds and scrutinizes data centers, and the AI complex broke. The selloff was not a routine product cycle: it was the market demanding proof that hyperscaler spending survives regulatory scrutiny and tighter financing. Oil's collapse and a strong payroll print muddied the macro signal, but the equity message was cleaner. AI infrastructure now carries a political discount and a financing test. The next leg depends on capex guidance and whether the scaled-back OpenAI round marks a funding reset or a pause.
