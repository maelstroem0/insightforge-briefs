The AI trade met the bond market

The week belonged to the collision between two crowded trades: long AI duration and short bond duration. Washington's hawkish chorus—Waller's half-point December call, Cook's dissent—pinned the US 10-year Treasury yield above 5%, while Nvidia's CEO exit, an SEC accounting probe and Anthropic's broken IPO turned AI momentum into a funding problem. By Friday the S&P 500 had fallen 3.7% and the Nasdaq 5.3%, while gold and bitcoin were liquidating as cash became the only bid. The tell was not the size of any single move but its coordination: the same dollar strength that crushed gold also drained crypto and non-US equities. Next week's test is whether the December FOMC path and Nvidia's disclosure satisfy the market—or force a broader de-risking.
