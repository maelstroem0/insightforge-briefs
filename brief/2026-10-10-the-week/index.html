Sovereign Term Premium Owned the Week

The week belonged to the long end. US 10-year and 30-year Treasury yields reached 5.365% and 5.72%, their highest since 2002, even as October Fed-hike odds fell and oil retreated; that divergence says the market is repricing sovereign term premium, not just the next Fed meeting. France's 10-year OAT yield near 5% and Japan's 30-year JGB yield at a record 4.235% confirmed it is global. The consequence: every risk asset now pays a higher discount rate. AI's credit boom — SpaceX's $40 billion GPU debt plan, AI-related issuance over half of net IG supply — is the next transmission channel, while Trump's pre-midterm Iran strike ban caps one inflation source. The consumer is the open question: nearly 20% of families behind on loans at end-2025. Next week's auctions and inflation data decide whether term premium tightens or loosens its grip.
