The Long End Reprices Everything

The week belonged to the long end. Sovereign yields from Treasuries to gilts to OATs repriced the term premium, and every asset had to answer to it: French spreads blew to a 2012-wide, India's Nifty extended its longest weekly losing streak since 2001, and the dollar's haven bid only broke when September payrolls missed. Nvidia's above-consensus fiscal 2028 guidance kept AI capex visible, and the G7 reserve release split the oil risk premium, but neither escaped the discount-rate story. The defining force is not growth or earnings; it is the price of government duration. Until long-end auctions and central-bank speak reveal a buyer, equity multiples and EM currencies remain the funding source.
