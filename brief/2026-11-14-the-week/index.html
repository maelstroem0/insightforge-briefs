The Discount Rate Broke the AI Trade

The week's defining force was the discount rate. October CPI and PPI ran hot, Fed officials kept tightening on the table, and Treasuries delivered their worst week since Liberation Day: the 10-year Treasury yield rose to 5.125% on Nov. 13 and the 30-year to 5.449%, while the dollar sat near a 19-month high. That repricing did not stop at bonds. Bitcoin fell below $70,000 on Friday, and the AI trade split into demand versus leadership: Nvidia's board ousted CEO Jensen Huang while Nebius cut its 2026 revenue outlook and raised capex, even as Cisco and Applied Materials reported strong AI orders. By Friday the question was not whether AI would be built, but at what cost of capital and under whose control. Next week's Fed meeting and Nvidia's interim leadership decide whether the repricing stabilizes or tightens.
