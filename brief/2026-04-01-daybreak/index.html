Dominant Theme

Geopolitical de-escalation in Iran drives a market rebound on reduced oil supply risks, but underlying macro and tech sector vulnerabilities persist, favoring commodities and AI infrastructure.

Key Risks

Prolonged Hormuz disruptions risk oil >$120/bbl (10-12M bpd shortfall). US-Europe political fractures strain NATO cohesion. Escalating US-China tech rivalry threatens supply chains. High US political polarization before 2026 midterms.

Strategic Shifts

Shifting global energy allegiances (China-Gulf, US LNG exports). Diversification of critical mineral supply chains away from China. Intensified AI competition fuels tech layoffs and infrastructure spend. Deglobalization pressures on multinational supply chains.

Opportunities

Long US LNG/defense on Europe's energy needs. Long cybersecurity firms on Iran cyber threats. Long AI infrastructure and Chinese semiconductors. Short leveraged crypto exchanges on instability. Overweight European defense stocks.
