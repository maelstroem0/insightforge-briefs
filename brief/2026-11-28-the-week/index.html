AI capex meets the rate cycle

Audio edition ~12 min · the week, in one voice

The week belonged to the collision between AI capex and the rate cycle. The hyperscaler buildout that powered the AI trade is now part of the inflation the Fed is fighting; when October PCE printed 3.8% year-over-year, the two stories became one. A Fed under Kevin Warsh that will not backstop equity drawdowns met an AI complex facing a higher discount rate and a governance discount together — Nvidia's CEO whiplash, the SEC probe, S&P's CreditWatch Negative. By Friday's buy-the-dip bounce off a correction, the market had repriced December and now January hikes. The rebound was a punt, not a resolution. The evidence that settles it — hyperscaler capex, Micron's December 17 earnings — is still ahead.
