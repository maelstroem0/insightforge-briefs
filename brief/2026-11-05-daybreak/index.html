29LIV Golf Nears Liquidation After BC Partners Pulls Relaunch Financing

BC Partners Credit scrapped plans to provide up to $300 million for a relaunched LIV Golf after too few players committed, leaving the Saudi PIF-backed league — which received more than $5 billion since 2022 — facing liquidation at a Thursday bankruptcy hearing in Trenton, New Jersey. About 30 players had signed up.

■Shifts professional golf's competitive balance toward the PGA and DP World Tours; PIF's exit is a sovereign-allocation signal, with no listed-equity read-through evidenced.
