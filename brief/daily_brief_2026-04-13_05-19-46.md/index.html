Dominant Theme

Geopolitical escalations in the Middle East and renewed inflation risks are driving global market volatility and commodity surges, forcing policymakers to recalibrate.

Key Risks

Hormuz blockade disrupting 20% global oil supply (up to $120/bbl). US inflation sustained at 4%+ by energy shocks. Ukraine conflict prolonging European energy uncertainty. AI-driven cyber threats to financial infrastructure.

Strategic Shifts

Accelerating US energy export dominance. Reshoring of critical industries like aluminum. China's AI and green tech export leadership. Decoupling of global supply chains for defense.

Opportunities

Long energy, defense, and US industrial equities. Short global equities/bonds, especially EU. Overweight US dollar. Alpha in cyber and specialized AI infrastructure. Long EM infrastructure (Egypt).
