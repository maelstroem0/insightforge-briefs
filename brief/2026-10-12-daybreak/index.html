A June strike at the same site killed 23 workers, and Egypt's border force seized 86 tonnes of gold ore in June.

36Migrant Boat Capsizes Off Djibouti, Killing at Least 139

At least 139 migrants died and dozens remain missing after a boat carrying 305 people from Djibouti to Yemen capsized near Obock on the night of October 8-9, the IOM said, revising its toll from 109 after 30 more bodies were recovered. Around 82 people were rescued; more than 3,700 fled Yemen by sea to Djibouti as of last week.
