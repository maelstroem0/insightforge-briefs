02Egypt's FRA Issues First Comprehensive Consumer Finance Rulebook

Egypt's Financial Regulatory Authority issued its first comprehensive guide unifying consumer finance regulation. The guide covers company establishment, licensing, governance, marketing, disclosure, customer protection, creditworthiness, Basel III solvency standards, cybersecurity, and debt collection rules for the non-bank consumer finance sector.

▲Regulatory clarity standardizes compliance costs and could support investor confidence and listed consumer-finance valuations; watch licensing uptake and new entrant filings as the next observable test.
