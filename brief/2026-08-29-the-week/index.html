The Long End Put the Fed on Trial

Audio edition ~12 min · the week, in one voice

This week was one story told three ways: who stands behind America's $40 trillion debt, and at what price. Bessent doubled long-end buybacks to $4 billion and watched the 30-year hit 5.34% anyway. Trump moved to fire Governor Lisa Cook and the Supreme Court let him — repricing Fed independence itself into the term premium. And Warsh's Jackson Hole debut, calling 3.7% PCE inflation 'preoccupying,' pushed September hike odds to 60% while gold shed 3%. Fiscal suppression, political capture, and hawkish rhetoric are fighting over the same bond, and the long end refused to be bought. The trade that follows: duration risk is now political risk, so own short-dated Treasuries and gold against it, and treat every 30-year auction and Cook court filing as a positioning event, not noise.
