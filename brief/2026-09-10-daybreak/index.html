03IAEA Refers Iran to UN Security Council as US Tests Deep-Strike Capability at Pickaxe Mountain

The IAEA board passed a resolution with 23 votes in favour, 3 against and 8 abstentions referring Iran to the UN Security Council for non-compliance — the first such move in 20 years — citing lack of access to bombed facilities and 440kg of 60% enriched uranium. Satellite imagery shows a construction surge at Iran's Pickaxe Mountain site, buried 140 miles south of Tehran, and the Pentagon awarded a $1.2 million emergency contract to test subterranean strike capabilities.

▼Escalation risk premium rises for oil and defense; Russia and China's veto limits UN sanction probability, so the price-setting channel is military, not diplomatic. Watch IAEA access reports and Gulf shipping insurance rates.

06EU Commission Proposes Buy-European Preference in €2.6 Trillion Public Procurement Market

The European Commission proposed overhauling public procurement to let EU states exclude Chinese companies from state, local-authority and public-enterprise tenders, ending the dominance of the price-only criterion blamed for favoring Chinese imports. The EU public-procurement market is worth 2.6 trillion euros annually, 15% of GDP; the reform is voluntary (opt-in) at member-state level.

▲Conditional positive read-through for EU construction, infrastructure and heavy-equipment suppliers gaining share as non-EU bidders are disqualified. Transmission is opt-in and slow — watch member-state transposition and the first tenders applying the rules.
