06NYT, OpenAI and Microsoft Argue Landmark Copyright Case—Training Economics on Trial

The New York Times, OpenAI and Microsoft presented arguments in the landmark copyright suit over training models on copyrighted content, Axios reports. A ruling for the Times disrupts AI business models via licensing costs; a ruling for OpenAI undermines publishers' content monetization. Judge Stein's ruling is the next test.

▲Binary catalyst for two sectors: an adverse ruling inflates training-data costs across frontier labs while a favorable one cuts publisher licensing revenue. Position around Judge Stein's ruling timing, not headlines.
